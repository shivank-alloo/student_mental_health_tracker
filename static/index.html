How does the ML model work?

We use a Scikit-Learn RandomForestClassifier trained on simulated student lifestyle data to predict burnout likelihood.

Is my data private?

Yes, all your logs are saved locally in a SQLite database file (`mental_health.db`). It is not sent to external servers.

Why did my productivity drop?

Typically, study sessions longer than 8 hours without adequate sleep result in strongly penalized productivity scores to encourage breaks.
